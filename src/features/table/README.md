# Playable table

`Table.tsx` mounts and disposes the playable desk. `scene/table-scene.js` uses the shared furniture builder for its rounded top and steel materials. The tabletop width and length come from the physics engine through the scene dependencies. `original-bench-texture.js` paints a light school-bench grain with worn student notes and hand-sketched desk, book, pencil and paper-plane doodles directly onto the playable tabletop. The surface remains matte and has no duplicate writing overlay. Surrounding furniture keeps lighter incidental marks. Texture changes update their wood materials without rebuilding character rigs or lighting.

Background furniture, walls, posters, floor and environment textures are separately owned by `src/features/classroom`. Coordinate physical table-size changes with the physics/controller owner: spawn positions, camera framing and character reach must remain consistent.

`bench-handwriting.js` draws original stroke lettering with distinct signature, ballpoint, pencil and carved profiles; it uses no browser or downloaded fonts. Notes include a name/visit tag, school batch, lunch/homework jokes and a maths scribble. Existing illustrations stay in place alongside a half-finished noughts-and-crosses game, a smiley, stars and an arrow-through-heart. Reference themes come from [photographs of a carved school desk](https://www.doeandhope.com/products/an-interesting-1920s-graffitied-oak-school-desk-lid) and [Rufo's documented study of student tabletop drawings](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1259972.pdf); the letters and drawings are original, not copied image assets. Update the artwork revision in the canvas cache key whenever the lettering or drawing changes.

Wood canvases are expanded to power-of-two dimensions before upload so WebGL 1 cannot silently reduce their detail. `bench-texture-filter.js` configures hardware-supported anisotropic filtering (up to 16×) and trilinear mipmaps on all bench surfaces to retain angled grain while avoiding distant shimmer.

The finished CPU canvas is cached per world, resolution, aspect ratio, finish and markings. Initial table/chair/classroom construction paints the matching surface once instead of four times. Each owner still creates and disposes its own GPU map. World disposal clears the canvas cache; changing resolution or finish selects a different entry.

`src/config/graphics.ts` owns per-quality wood resolution and maximum anisotropy; `settings.ts` owns the initial oak/maple/walnut choice. Values reach both the main desk and surrounding furniture through `config.ts`. `tests/furniture.test.js` checks texture dimensions/filtering; `tests/scene.test.js` checks matching materials and finish changes without disposing another scene owner's resources. See [Testing](../../../docs/testing.md) for visual sharpness checks. The archived user wood photograph is documented separately in `public/textures/furniture/CREDITS.md` and is not the active bench finish.
